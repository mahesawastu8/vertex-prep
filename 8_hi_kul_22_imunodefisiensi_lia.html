Pada bagian ini, dr. LIA membahas transisi penting antara slide 1 sampai 11. Perhatikan alur logisnya pada slide asli di sebelah kanan:

Poin Kunci & Fakta Laboratorium (Gunakan Mode Uji Ingatan M)

  • Slide 2: Define immunodeficiency 2
  • Slide 4: Immunodeficiency • = immune deficiency • Is a state in which the immune system’s ability to fight infectious disease is entirely absent or compromised • It is the absence or failure of normal function of one or more elements of the immune system Primary Secondary
  • Slide 6: Primary Immunodeficiency • A group of disorder characterized by an impaired ability to produce normal immune response • Cause: mutation in genes involves in the development & function of immune organs, cells & molecules • Genetically determined • Usually disease of infancy & childhood • 70% males (5:1 males in children; 1:1 in adults)
  • Slide 7: Primary Immunodeficiency • Inherited as 6 X-linked [5-affect lymphocytes & 1 affect phagocyte] or autosomal disorders, either dominant or recessive • gene defects lead to missing enzymes, developmental arrest in immune differentiation, absent or non-functional proteins, abnormal DNA repair, altered signal transduction, impairment of cell-to-cell and intracellular communications

Common Variable immunodeficiency (CVI) Normal amount

Poin Kunci & Fakta Laboratorium (Gunakan Mode Uji Ingatan M)

  • Slide 23: Common Variable immunodeficiency (CVI) • Normal amount of B cell but disfunctional • B cell fail to receive signaling from T lymphocytes, so they can not to be plasma cell • Ig decrease (low IgG and IgA) • Impaired antibody response • Associated autoimmune disease • Function of CMI is normal or defective • Cause: unknown • Males = females • Recurrent infection between 15-35 yrs • Recurrent infection involving the ears, eyes, sinusitis, nose, bronchi, lungs, etc • The organisms coomnly found in these infections are bacteria that often cause pneumonia (H
  • Slide 24: Disgammaglobulinemia • Decrease amount of selective Ig • = IgA deficiency • IgA is low, but IgG and IgM normal/increase • IgA deficiency is most common(1:700) • Recurrent mucosal infection: GIT or RTI • Result from failure in terminal differentiation of B cells • Increase incidence of autoimmune, malignancies and alergy • Anti-convulsant drugs (phenytoin) may cause deficiency • Tx: antibiotic broad spectrum B-cell Immunodeficiency
  • Slide 25: Immunodeficiency Primary Complement NK cell IFN and lysozim Phagocyte B cell T cell
  • Slide 26: DiGeorge Syndrome • = congenital thymic aplasia/hypoplasia • Congenital disorder, no heredity • No T cell [low] in blood, lien and lymph node • Normal B cell, plasma cell and Ig • Abnormalities in structure derived from 3rd and 4th pharyngeal pouches • Involve thymus and parathyroid gland • Symptoms: hypocalcemia within 24 hour after birth, congenital heart and renal disease • Characterystic: neonatal tetany (absence of parathyroid), fish mouth appearance • Recurrent infection of virus, bacteria, fungi and protozoa • Tx: thymic fetal transplants (<14 days to avoid Graft versus Host reaction) T-cell Immunodeficiency

Bab 05 · Slide 45 s.d. 55 Inti UAB 8 (HI)

Nature review. Immunology. Volume March 2009.185

Pada bagian ini, dr. LIA membahas transisi penting antara slide 45 sampai 55. Perhatikan alur logisnya pada slide asli di sebelah kanan:

Poin Kunci & Fakta Laboratorium (Gunakan Mode Uji Ingatan M)

  • Slide 47: Cancer/Chemotherapy • Both disease & treatment can cause immunosuppresion • Neutropenia: <1
  • Slide 48: Drugs: corticosteroid • Glucocorticoids (corticosteroids) have inhibitory effect on T cells and B cells ⮚Depletion of CD4 cells ⮚Monocytopenia ⮚Decreased in circulating eosinophils and basophils ⮚Inhibition of T cell activation and B cell maturation ⮚Inhibit cytokine synthesis • Infections depend on route of administration, dose and duration of therapy • Prolonged CMI suppression imortant for opportunistic infection to occur • Delayed wound healing and wound infection steroid interfere with fibroblas proliferaation & collagen synthesis
  • Slide 49: Drugs METHOTREXATE • Prolonged use for treatment reduces immunoglobulin synthesis • Structural analogue of folic acid • Blocks folic acid dependent synthetic pathways essential for DNA s CYCOLOSPORIN • Have severe effects on T cell signaling and functions • It binds to immunophilins which are believed to have a critical role in signal transduction • Also inhibit IL 2 dependent signal transduction
  • Slide 51: Initial evaluation of possible immunodeficiency • Make sure that what seems to be infections are not really: atopy, allergy, asthma • Exclude other condition • Hold off on any live viral vaccine or transfusions until situation well defined • Document that there have been multiple infection • Look for other, non-immune feature of immunodeficiency: rash, hypocalcemia, facial characteristics • Family history
