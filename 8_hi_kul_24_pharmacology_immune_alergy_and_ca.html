TAXAN Paclitaxel (Diterpenoid from Taxus brevifolia),

Pada bagian ini, dr. SAN membahas transisi penting antara slide 7 sampai 12. Perhatikan alur logisnya pada slide asli di sebelah kanan:

Poin Kunci & Fakta Laboratorium (Gunakan Mode Uji Ingatan M)

  • Slide 7: TAXAN • Paclitaxel (Diterpenoid from Taxus brevifolia), Docetaxel (semisynthetic from Taxus baccata) • Mechanism action: bind to microtubules thereby preventing cell division and causing cancer cell death • preparation : • Paclitaxel : injection powder (6 mg/mL), infus 3-24h every 3 week • Docetaxel : 100 mg infus 1 hour, every 3 weeks, for 4-6 cycle • therapeutic use: • ovarian and prostat cancer, breast cancer, non-small cell lung cancer, and AIDS-associated Kaposi's sarcoma • side effect: • Paclitaxel : severe allergic reactions such as anaphylaxis, spinal cord suppression, left ventricular dysfunction, pneumonitis
  • Slide 8: Mechlorethamine (Bendamustine) • Mechanism action: transfer alkyl residues and form DNA cross- links, causing inhibition of DNA synthesis and function • preparation : injection 25 mg dan 100 mg
  • Slide 9: Cisplatin • Platinum substance • Mechanism action: Form a cross bond with Guanin base on the Double Helix DNA chain so disrupts the transcription and replication of DNA, also causes toxicity in cells by inducing oxidative stress • preparation : Vial 50 mg, 50-100 mg iv infusion for 6-8 h every 3-4 w • therapeutic use: lung cancer, breast cancer, ovarian and testis cancer, bladder cancer, head and neck cancer, GIT cancer → the most widely used • side effect: nausea and vomiting, nephrotoxicity, myelosuppression, ototoxicity, pheripheral neuropathy • Mechanisms of resistance: DNA repair enzymes
  • Slide 10: CYTOSTATIC ANTIBIOTICS Mechanism action: insert themselves into the DNA double strand, lead to strand breakage a

Pada bagian ini, dr. SAN membahas transisi penting antara slide 13 sampai 18. Perhatikan alur logisnya pada slide asli di sebelah kanan:

Poin Kunci & Fakta Laboratorium (Gunakan Mode Uji Ingatan M)

  • Slide 13: ANTIMETABOLITE/ INSERTION OF INCORRECT BUILDING BLOCKS a
  • Slide 14: 5-fluorouracil • Unnatural nucleobases • Mechanism action: inhibit enzyme thymidylate synthase, resulting in failure of pyrimidine synthesis and replace the uracil group in RNA, resulting in failure of RNA replication • Preparation: IV, 400 mg/m2 infusion, followed by 2,800 mg/m2/46d, repeated every 2w • therapeutic use: breast cancer and malignancies of the gastrointestinal tract (cancer of the nasopharynx, stomach, colon, rectum, and pancreas) • side effect: mielosuppression, mucositis, stomatitis, diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, hand foot and mouth disease, neurological dysfunction (cerebellar ataxia, somnolence, seizures), and cardiac dysfunction (angina pectoris, myocardial ischemia)
  • Slide 15: Cytarabine • nucleosides with incorrect sugars • Mechanism action: pyrimidine analog that incorporates DNA, thereby interfering pyrimidine synthesis
  • Slide 16: IMMUNOSUPPRESSIVES AGENT • The goal of suppression of immune responses: • prevention of transplant rejection • treatment of autoimmune disorders • Risk: weakened defenses against infectious pathogens and a long-term increase in the risk of neoplasms
